Benefits

Why Legacy is the right tool for Bitcoin inheritance.

Benefits
  • Encrypt and decrypt without internet connection.
  • Excludes lawyers, executors, and other trusted third-parties.
  • Consistent with the Bitcoin P2P ethos.
  • Choose your own encryption keys.
  • Can be used completely anonymously.
  • Create as many two-part passwords as you like.
  • Opensource. Transparent.
Things to consider
  • Legacy does NOT store any data.
  • You need BOTH keys and the encrypted seed phrase to decrypt.
  • If any one of these pieces of information are lost, the Bitcoin is irretrievable.
  • You have sole responsibility for maintaining your keys and encrypted seed phrase.
  • Keeping a working copy of "Legacy-offline" is also recommended to decrypt the seed phrase.
  • This tool is intended to be used in conjunction with a Deadman Switch to deliver the Benefactor Key and Encrypted Seed Phrase.
  • The security of this protocol depends entirely on the strength of your keys. Use long, random, unpredictable strings — not memorable passphrases.
