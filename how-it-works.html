How It Works

Four steps to secure your Bitcoin inheritance.

1
The Benefactor downloads the html file from GitHub. Use Legacy's offline encryption protocol to choose a Benefactor Key and a Beneficiary Key, then encrypt your seed phrase. Together, these two keys form the single password required for decryption.
2
The Benefactor gives the Beneficiary Key to the Beneficiary to keep safe.
3
The Benefactor Key and the Encrypted Seed Phrase are packed into a Deadman Switch whereby, upon the death of the Benefactor, an email is sent to the Beneficiary containing the Benefactor Key and the Encrypted Seed Phrase. See Deadman Switch options.
4
Finally, the Beneficiary enters both keys into the Legacy decrypt data fields and the seed phrase will be decrypted and ready to recover the Bitcoin.
